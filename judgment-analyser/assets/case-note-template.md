# Case Note Template

The output structure. Follow this order. Omit no section — where a section does not
apply, say why rather than deleting it.

## Notes on Producing the .docx

- Times New Roman 12pt, 1.5 spacing, justified body, 1 inch margins, unless
  `firm-context.md` says otherwise
- Headings numbered as above; do not use decorative styling
- Tables keep their borders — they are read, not skimmed
- Quoted holdings in indented block quotes with the paragraph cite immediately after
- Filename: `Case Note - [Short Case Name] - [Court] - [YYYY-MM-DD].docx`

## One-Page Brief Variant

If the user selected "one-page brief" in Step 2, produce sections 1 (three lines),
2, 4 (ratio statement only), 6.1, 6.4, and 7.1–7.2 and 7.7. The subsequent-history
caveat is never dropped, whatever the length.

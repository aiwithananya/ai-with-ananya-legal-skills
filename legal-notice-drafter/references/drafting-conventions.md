# Drafting Conventions — Advocate's Legal Notice, India

The notice issues from the advocate, on the advocate's letterhead, under instructions
from the client. The advocate speaks in the first person; the client is referred to
throughout as "my client", never by name after the introduction.

---

## Structure

### 1. Header block

```
Ref. No. [____]                                          Date: [____]

BY REGISTERED POST WITH ACKNOWLEDGEMENT DUE
BY SPEED POST
BY EMAIL: [____]
```

List every mode actually used. The modes stated here must match the service memo.

### 2. Addressee block

```
To,

1. [Full legal name]
   [Constitution, e.g. a company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013]
   [Registered office address]

2. [Second noticee]
   [Capacity — e.g. Director, arrayed in his personal capacity under
    Section 141 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881]
   [Address]
```

Where a company is a noticee, serve at the registered office as shown on the MCA
record, not merely at the correspondence address on the invoices.

### 3. Subject line

One sentence. Statutory provision, the act complained of, and the demand.

> Sub: Legal notice under Section 138 of the Negotiable Instruments Act, 1881
> calling upon you to pay ₹[____] towards the dishonoured cheque bearing
> No. [____] dated [____]

### 4. Salutation

`Madam/Sir,` — or `Madam,` / `Sir,` where the noticee is an identified individual.

### 5. Introduction

> Under instructions from and on behalf of my client, [full legal name],
> [constitution], having its registered office at [address] (hereinafter referred to
> as "my client"), I address you as under:

### 6. Recitals

Numbered paragraphs, each opening with "That". One fact per paragraph. Chronological.

> 1. That my client is engaged in the business of [____].
> 2. That by an agreement dated [____] executed between my client and yourself, [____].
> 3. That in terms of Clause [____] of the said agreement, which reads as under:
>
>    "[verbatim quotation]"

Quote every operative document verbatim and indent the quotation. Do not paraphrase
a clause, a cheque endorsement, a return memo or an email and present it as the
document's language.

**No annexures.** A legal notice is not a pleading and carries no annexures,
schedules or enclosures. Identify every document in the body of the recital itself,
with enough particularity that it is unmistakable — its nature, date, number and
parties.

> That by a Supply Agreement dated 12th August 2025 executed between my client and
> yourself, my client agreed to supply fabric upon the terms recorded therein.

Never write "annexed hereto and marked Annexure A", "a copy whereof is enclosed", or
any similar formulation. Do not add an enclosures line to the signature block.

### 7. Statutory basis

A separate paragraph identifying the provision relied on and why it applies on these
facts. Take the language from the profile file. Do not embellish it.

### 8. The demand

The operative paragraph. It must be unmistakable. It carries **no heading** — it is
simply the next numbered paragraph in the sequence, continuing the flow of the
letter.

> I, therefore, call upon you to pay to my client a sum of ₹[____] (Rupees [____]
> only) together with interest thereon at the rate of [____]% per annum from
> [____] till realisation, within [____] days of receipt of this notice.

Where the statute fixes the demand — the cheque amount under s.138, for instance —
that amount must appear as a distinct and separately identifiable figure even if a
larger total is also claimed.

### 9. Consequence

> Failing compliance within the said period, my client shall be constrained to
> initiate [proceedings under Section ____ of the ____ Act] against you before the
> appropriate forum, entirely at your risk as to costs and consequences.

State only proceedings the client has instructed and is willing to bring. In an
intellectual property notice this paragraph carries statutory exposure — see the
groundless threats note in the profile file.

### 10. Costs of notice

> The cost of this notice is ₹[____], which my client is entitled to recover from
> you in addition to the sums claimed above.

### 11. Reservation

> This notice is issued without prejudice to any other rights and remedies available
> to my client under law or in equity, all of which are expressly reserved.

### 12. Record clause

> A copy of this notice is retained in my office for future reference and record.

### 13. Signature block

```
Yours faithfully,


[Advocate name]
Advocate
Enrolment No. [____]
[Office address]
[Email] | [Phone]
```

Nothing follows the signature block. No enclosures line, no annexure list, no
distribution list.

---

## No section headings in the notice

The numbered sections above are guidance for the drafter. They are **not** headings
in the document. The notice runs as continuous numbered paragraphs from the
salutation to the signature block, with nothing between them except the paragraph
numbers themselves.

Do not insert "DEMAND", "FACTS", "BACKGROUND", "STATUTORY BASIS", "RELIEF" or any
other heading, bolded run-in or underlined label into the body. A legal notice reads
as a letter, and a letter does not have section headings.

---

## Register and usage

- Formal but not archaic. Drop "wherein", "hereinbefore", "the said noticee herein"
  where a plain word does the work. "The said agreement" is standard and acceptable.
- Third person for the noticee is wrong. Address the noticee as "you" throughout.
- Amounts in figures followed by words: `₹5,00,000/- (Rupees Five Lakh only)`.
- Indian digit grouping: `₹1,50,00,000/-`, not `₹15,000,000`.
- Dates as `15th March 2026` or `15.03.2026`, consistently, one style throughout.
- Never assert a fact the client has not stated. Where a fact is believed but not
  confirmed, write "my client is informed and believes".
- No adjectives about the noticee's character. "You have failed and neglected to pay"
  is the strongest formulation the document needs.

## Page formatting

Follow `references/firm-context.md`. Absent a firm standard: A4, Times New Roman 12
point, 1.5 line spacing, justified, 1 inch margins, paragraphs numbered continuously,
page numbers bottom centre, and the advocate's initials at the foot of every page
where the notice runs beyond two pages.

## Draft header

Every draft produced by this skill carries, above the letterhead block:

```
[DRAFT — REQUIRES ADVOCATE REVIEW BEFORE ISSUE]
```

It is removed by the advocate, not by the skill.

# Writ Petition Structure Guide (Article 226/227)

Distilled from the firm's sample CWP. A complete writ petition is assembled in
this order. Reproduce every component; do not drop any unless the user says so.

Mandamus = compelling a public authority to perform a duty / declaring inaction
or an act unlawful. Certiorari = quashing an order, notification, or proceeding
of a subordinate authority/tribunal. Most matters combine both (quash the
impugned order + direct the authority). The sample is a certiorari + declaration
petition; keep the same skeleton for mandamus, swapping the relief verbs.

## 1. Cause Title (repeated as a header block on each preliminary section)
```
Before the Hon'ble <High Court>, <Seat>
Civil Writ Petition No. ______ of <year>
<Petitioner name>                                   ....Petitioner
Versus
<Lead respondent> & Others                          ....Respondents
```

## 2. Synopsis
Narrative overview (4-6 paragraphs): who the petitioner is, the impugned
action/order, the legal provisions engaged, why it is illegal/arbitrary, and the
core relief sought. Closes with the counsel block, Place and Date.

## 3. List of Dates & Events
Two-column chronology: date (left) -> event (right). Each event that relies on a
document cites "(Annexure P-__) (Page No. __-__)". Ends with the "Hence, the
present petition is preferred ..." line, then counsel block, Place, Date.

## 4. Memo of Parties
Full particulars. Petitioner: name, through whom (proprietor/director), age,
address, and contact/ID particulars if given. Respondents: numbered, each with
"through its <designating officer>", office and address. Counsel block, Place,
Date.

## 5. Petition Heading + Relief Clause
```
Civil Writ Petition under Article 226 & 227 Constitution of India, 1950
seeking issuance of:
   i)   Writ of Certiorari quashing <impugned order/notification> ...
   ii)  Declaration that <provision> is ultra vires ... (if applicable)
   iii) In alternative: Read down <provision> ... (if applicable)
   iv)  Writ of Mandamus directing <authority> to <act> ... (if applicable)
   And
   Pass such or similar order as deemed fit ...
```
Follow with the interim-relief prayer ("during pendency ... operation of impugned
order may kindly be stayed ...").

## 6. Respectfully Showeth (numbered facts and grounds)
Arabic-numbered paragraphs, each opening with "That ...":
- Para 1: jurisdiction — petitioner's status, respondents' status, and that the
  cause of action arose within the Court's territorial jurisdiction, invoking the
  extraordinary writ jurisdiction under Articles 226 & 227.
- Para 2 onward: facts, statutory background, and provisions reproduced verbatim
  in indented blocks, each fact/document tied to its annexure + page.
- Sub-headings (short italic/bold lines) break the narrative by theme, e.g.
  "Notifications extending time limits ...", "Merits of the Case".

## 7. Grounds
Lettered A, B, C ... and continuing AA, BB, CC ... after Z. Each ground opens
"That ..." and states one legal proposition, tied where possible to a
constitutional article, statutory provision, or cited judgment
("The Hon'ble <Court> in <case> (<citation>) held that ...").

## 8. Questions of Law
A numbered paragraph ("That from above the following questions of law arise for
the consideration of this Hon'ble Court:") followed by roman-numbered questions
(i), (ii), (iii) ... each phrased "Whether ...".

## 9. Alternative Remedy / Maintainability
Paragraph addressing the statutory appeal (e.g. Section 107 GST Act) and why the
writ is still maintainable (vires challenge, jurisdictional error, breach of
natural justice, etc.).

## 10. No Similar Petition
"That no such or similar petition has been filed by the Petitioner before this
Hon'ble Court or the Supreme Court or any other Court of law in India."

## 11. Prayer
```
It is, therefore, humbly prayed that this Hon'ble Court be pleased to summon the
record and:
   i)   Issue Writ of ______ quashing ______;
   ii)  Declare / Read down ______ (if applicable);
   iii) Issue Writ of Mandamus directing ______ (if applicable);
   iv)  Exempt the Petitioner from filing certified copies of the Annexures;
   v)   Permit the Petitioner to file Annexures in the enclosed form/font/size/margin;
   vi)  Pass such or similar order as deemed fit ...
```
Mirror the relief clause in Section 5 but in operative "Issue/Declare/Direct" form.

## 12. Verification, Affidavit, Counsel block (standard closers)
- Signature line for the petitioner / counsel, Place and Date.
- Verification clause ("Verified that the contents of paras __ to __ are true to
  my knowledge and paras __ to __ are believed to be true on legal advice ...").
- Supporting affidavit of the petitioner.
Draft these as placeholders clearly marked for the petitioner's confirmation of
which paragraphs are true-to-knowledge vs. on-advice.

## Assembly note
Preliminary components (Synopsis, List of Dates, Memo of Parties) each carry the
full cause-title header. The main petition body runs 5 -> 12 as one continuous
document. Use page-break separation between components in the .docx.

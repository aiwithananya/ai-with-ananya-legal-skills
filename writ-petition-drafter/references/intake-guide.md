# Guided Intake Guide

Run intake in three passes: FACTS, LAW, RESEARCH. Before asking anything, read
any uploaded brief/documents and any matter `claude.md`, then ask ONLY for the
gaps. Confirm what you extracted rather than re-asking. Keep total questions
reasonable (aim 5-8 across the passes; batch related items).

## Pass 1 — FACTS
Collect / confirm:
- Court and seat (default from firm-context, if set; otherwise ask. Any Indian
  High Court).
- Petitioner: full name, capacity (proprietor/director/individual), age, address,
  contact/ID particulars.
- Respondents: each authority, the officer it acts "through", and address.
  (Union of India, State, and the assessing/adjudicating authority are typical.)
- The impugned action: order / notification / notice — number, date, issuing
  authority, and what it does. Mark each with its intended Annexure P-__.
- Chronology: dated events for the List of Dates & Events, each with its document.
- Whether interim stay of the impugned order is sought.

## Pass 2 — LAW
Collect / confirm:
- Nature of relief: certiorari (quash), mandamus (direct), declaration/ultra
  vires, read-down, or a combination.
- Statutory provisions engaged (Act, section, rule) — with exact citations.
- Constitutional articles relied on (e.g. 14, 19(1)(g), 21, 300A, 226/227).
- Which provisions must be reproduced verbatim in the body. Ask the user to
  paste the exact statutory text, or confirm you may pull it — if pulled, mark
  "[STATUTORY TEXT TO BE VERIFIED]" until confirmed.
- The core legal propositions that will become the lettered Grounds.

## Pass 3 — RESEARCH POINTS
Collect / confirm:
- Case authorities relied on: party names, court, and citation. If a citation is
  missing or approximate, record it and mark "[CITATION TO BE VERIFIED]".
- The proposition each authority supports and where it should sit (which ground).
- Any statutory-interpretation or vires arguments (e.g. delegated legislation
  exceeding its parent section) to develop as grounds.
- The questions of law that flow from the grounds.
- Maintainability / alternative-remedy angle (e.g. why an available statutory
  appeal does not bar the writ).

## After intake
Produce a short intake summary and get the user's confirmation before drafting.
Any fact, date, citation, page number, or statutory text not supplied and not
verifiable is a clearly-marked placeholder — never invented.

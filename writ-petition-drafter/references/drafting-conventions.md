# Drafting Conventions (house style)

Mirror the firm's sample CWP exactly. These are the non-negotiable conventions.

## Paragraph style
- Every substantive paragraph opens with "That ...".
- Formal register: "Hon'ble", "the Petitioner", "the Respondent(s)",
  "impugned order", "ultra vires", "Respectfully Showeth", "It is humbly prayed".
- Refer to parties by role (Petitioner/Respondent No. __), not personal names,
  in the body after they are once identified.

## Numbering
- Facts in "Respectfully Showeth": Arabic (1, 2, 3 ...).
- Grounds: letters A, B, C ... then AA, BB, CC ... after Z.
- Relief clauses and prayer: roman (i), (ii), (iii) ...
- Questions of law: roman (i), (ii), (iii) ...

## Annexures
- Cite inline as "(Annexure P-__) (Page No. __-__)".
- Number annexures P-1, P-2 ... in the order first referenced.
- Maintain an internal annexure list so numbering stays consistent across the
  Synopsis, List of Dates, and body.
- If page numbers are unknown, insert "(Page No. ___)" as a marked placeholder —
  never invent page ranges.

## Statutory / case material
- Reproduce statutory provisions VERBATIM in an indented block. Never paraphrase
  the text of a section, rule, or notification.
- Cite provisions precisely: "Section 16(2)(c) of the Haryana Goods & Services
  Tax Act, 2017", "Rule 42 & 43 of the HGST Rules, 2017", "Article 14 of the
  Constitution of India".
- Case citations: "The Hon'ble <Court> in <Party v. Party> (<neutral/report
  citation>) held that ...". If the exact citation is not supplied, mark it
  "[CITATION TO BE VERIFIED]" — do not fabricate a citation.

## Cause title
- Full court name and seat: e.g. "Before the Hon'ble Punjab & Haryana High Court,
  Chandigarh".
- Petitioner and Respondent lines right-aligned to "....Petitioner" /
  "....Respondents".

## Counsel / closing block
```
Place: <seat>
Date: <date>
                                  <Advocate>   <Advocate>   <Advocate>
                                            Advocates
                                  Counsels for the Petitioner
```

## Output
- Produce a Word (.docx) file. Preliminary components separated by page breaks.
- Use the docx skill (Read its SKILL.md) to generate the file with proper
  headings, indentation, and page breaks.
- Filename: "<Petitioner short name> - CWP <year>.docx".

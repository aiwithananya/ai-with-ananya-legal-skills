# Legal Notice Style

Use unless the user uploads their letterhead or a previous notice. Their format always wins on form.

- **Page:** A4, 1 inch margins. If a letterhead is supplied, start the body below it and leave space for its footer.
- **Font:** Times New Roman 12 pt, 1.5 line spacing, justified.
- **Top block:** reference number (left) and date (right) on one line. Then "BY REGISTERED POST AD / SPEED POST / EMAIL" (list each mode used), bold.
- **Addressee:** "To," then name, designation and full address. Multiple addressees numbered.
- **Subject:** "Subject:" in bold, one line summarising the notice, underlined.
- **Salutation:** "Sir / Madam," or "Dear Sir,".
- **Opening:** "Under instructions from and on behalf of my client, [name and address], I hereby serve upon you the following legal notice:".
- **Body:** numbered paragraphs 1, 2, 3, continuous. Quoted contract clauses indented.
- **Demand:** its own paragraph, often bold: "I, therefore, call upon you to ... within [ ] days of receipt of this notice".
- **Consequence and reservation:** next paragraphs, then "A copy of this notice has been retained in my office for record and further action."
- **Signature:** right-aligned: "Yours faithfully," then advocate's name, "Advocate", enrolment number if used.
- **Footer:** page number bottom centre if more than one page.

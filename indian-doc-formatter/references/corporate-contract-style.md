# Corporate Contract Style (default for agreements)

Use unless the user uploads a sample or gives other instructions. A sample always wins on form.

- **Page:** A4, margins 2.54 cm (1 inch) all round, portrait.
- **Font:** Times New Roman 12 pt body; headings same font, bold.
- **Spacing:** 1.15 line spacing for body, 6 pt after each paragraph. Clause headings keep with next.
- **Alignment:** justified body; headings left.
- **Cover / title:** agreement title centred, bold, all caps. Date and place line beneath.
- **Parties block:** each party in its own paragraph: name in bold caps, constitution (company incorporated under the Companies Act, 2013 / LLP / individual), registered address, "(hereinafter referred to as the "**[Short Name]**", which expression shall, unless repugnant to the context, include its successors and permitted assigns)". "AND" centred between parties.
- **Recitals:** "WHEREAS" then lettered (A), (B), (C). Final recital: "NOW, THEREFORE, in consideration of the mutual covenants...".
- **Numbering:** one multi-level list: 1. / 1.1 / 1.1.1 / (a) / (i). Level 1 headings bold caps. Never type numbers by hand.
- **Definitions clause:** clause 1. Each defined term in bold and inside double quotes on first definition, alphabetical order. Later uses keep the same capitalisation, not bold.
- **Cross-references:** "Clause 4.2" as a Word REF field to a bookmark on that clause.
- **Schedules:** each on a new page, heading "SCHEDULE 1" centred bold, title beneath.
- **Execution block:** "IN WITNESS WHEREOF the Parties have executed this Agreement on the date first written above." Two-column table: "For and on behalf of [Party]", signature line, Name, Designation, then Witness 1 and 2 with name and address.
- **Footer:** "Page X of Y", centred. Optional document title, left, 9 pt.
- **Stamp paper note:** leave the top third of page 1 blank only if the user says it will be printed on stamp paper.

# Litigation Pleading Style (petitions, plaints, complaints, applications)

Use unless the user uploads a sample or names a court whose rules they paste. A sample always wins on form. Court rules differ (Supreme Court, each High Court, district courts): if the user names a court, ask whether they have its format or a sample.

- **Page:** A4 (some courts still require legal size; ask if unsure). Left margin 4 cm (binding), right 2.5 cm, top and bottom 2.5 cm. One side of the page.
- **Font:** Times New Roman 14 pt body is common in High Courts; 12 pt in many district courts. Ask or follow the sample. Quotations from statutes or judgments indented and 12 pt.
- **Spacing:** double or 1.5 line spacing for body; single within block quotations.
- **Cause title:** centred: "IN THE HIGH COURT OF ... AT ...", jurisdiction line ("CIVIL WRIT JURISDICTION"), case number line with blanks ("W.P. (C) No. ____ of 20__"). Then "IN THE MATTER OF:" and the parties block with "...Petitioner(s)" and "...Respondent(s)" right-aligned, "VERSUS" centred.
- **Index and preliminaries:** if present, Index, Synopsis / List of Dates and Events, Memo of Parties each on a new page with the cause title repeated at the top.
- **Body opening:** "MOST RESPECTFULLY SHOWETH:" bold.
- **Paragraphs:** Arabic numbers 1, 2, 3, often opening "That". Sub-paragraphs (a), (b).
- **Grounds:** heading "GROUNDS", lettered A, B, C ... then AA, BB after Z (follow sample if different).
- **Annexures:** cited inline in bold, for example "(**Annexure P-1**)". Each annexure later has a cover sheet "ANNEXURE P-1" and a "True Copy" note.
- **Prayer:** heading "PRAYER", reliefs lettered (a), (b), ending "and pass such other order(s) as this Hon'ble Court may deem fit".
- **Signature block:** right-aligned: party / "Through" / counsel names / "Advocates for the Petitioner", with Place and Date on the left.
- **Verification and affidavit:** each on a new page; deponent block at the end.
- **Footer:** page number bottom centre.

# Intake Checklist: Section 138 Complaint

Ask in grouped batches. Mark anything not supplied as `[TO BE CONFIRMED: ...]`. Never invent a fact.

## Batch 1: Parties
- Complainant: full name, father's/husband's name or entity name, age (if individual), full address
- If the complainant is a company or firm: name of the authorised representative, and the board resolution or authority letter date
- Accused: full name or entity name, full address
- If the accused is a company or firm: names, designations and addresses of every director, partner or signatory to be made accused under S.141, and what each was in charge of

## Batch 2: The debt
- Nature of the transaction (loan, sale of goods, services, rent, settlement)
- Amount due and how it is made up
- Documents proving the debt (invoices, ledger, agreement, acknowledgment, emails, WhatsApp messages)

## Batch 3: The cheque
- Cheque number, date, amount
- Drawee bank and branch
- Account on which it was drawn (whose account)
- Who signed it
- Was it given for the debt above, or as security? (Ask plainly. A security cheque needs careful pleading.)

## Batch 4: Dishonour
- Date of presentation
- Complainant's bank and branch through which it was presented
- Date of the return memo, and the exact reason written on it
- Date the complainant received the return memo or information from the bank

## Batch 5: Demand notice
- Date of the legal demand notice
- Mode of dispatch (Speed Post, courier, email, WhatsApp) and tracking numbers
- Date of delivery, or the date it was returned and the endorsement (refused, unclaimed, left, insufficient address)
- Any reply from the accused, and its date
- Any part-payment after the notice

## Batch 6: Filing
- Court where it will be filed (exact cause-title wording)
- Planned date of filing
- Witnesses (usually the complainant or authorised representative, and a bank official if needed)

## Documents to list as annexures
Original cheque, return memo, bank statement entry, demand notice, postal receipts and tracking report, delivery proof or returned envelope, reply (if any), debt documents, authority letter or board resolution.

# Quality Review: Section 138 Filing Set

Run every check. Fix what you can; flag the rest at the top of the cover note.

## Timeline
- [ ] Cheque presented within six months or validity, whichever is earlier
- [ ] Notice sent within 30 days of receipt of the return memo
- [ ] 15-day period counted from the date of service of notice
- [ ] Complaint within one month of the cause of action, or a condonation application drafted and flagged
- [ ] Timeline table included with calendar dates

## Pleadings
- [ ] Legally enforceable debt pleaded with its source documents
- [ ] Exact return reason from the memo quoted
- [ ] Separate jurisdiction paragraph under S.142(2)
- [ ] For a company or firm: S.141 role pleaded for each accused person individually
- [ ] Prayer asks for summoning, trial and punishment; interim compensation under S.143A offered as a separate application
- [ ] Verification present and consistent with the affidavit

## Citations
- [ ] Heading follows the Citation Lock in `statutory-framework.md`
- [ ] No Cr.P.C. sections used for a post-1 July 2024 filing
- [ ] No section of the Bharatiya Sakshya Adhiniyam in the affidavit heading
- [ ] Every section number checked against the bare Act

## Consistency
- [ ] Names, cheque number, amount, dates identical across complaint, affidavit and both lists
- [ ] Every annexure cited in the complaint appears in the List of Documents, in the same order
- [ ] Format matches the uploaded sample or the chosen default

## Placeholders
- [ ] Every missing fact is marked `[TO BE CONFIRMED: ...]` and listed in the cover note
- [ ] Closing line present: "Draft for advocate review. Verify all facts, dates, annexures and statutory citations before filing."
